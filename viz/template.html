Communities in the follow graph

Widely-followed accounts, grouped by who follows them: two accounts land in the same community when the same people follow both. Communities nest: broad groups (bold) contain smaller sub-communities, and the groups that are mainly about left-leaning politics sit together under one umbrella. The audience groupings come from the follow graph; the political umbrella comes from a model's reading of each community's accounts and posts. Sub-communities will shift with a larger sample. Each dot is one of the most-followed accounts among a random sample of active users; nearby dots share an audience. Pick a community to highlight it.

Dot size = how many sampled active users follow the account. Position comes from t-SNE on audience overlap, so distances are only meaningful locally.

How the groups connect

Where each group's members point their follows. Read across a row: the share of that group's follows to core accounts that go to each group. The groups are not islands, and links are often one-way: gaming members follow the celebrity and comedy group far more than it follows them back. Click a cell to see posts by the accounts being followed.

What each community talks about

For each community, what its members post about. Members are sampled active users, assigned to the community they follow most; the view starts with each topic's share compared with the average across communities, which shows what makes each community distinctive.
